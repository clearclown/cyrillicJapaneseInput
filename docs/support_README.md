## 🌐 About Pismo

**Pismo** (Писмо) is a keyboard application that enables typing Japanese (Hiragana/Katakana) using Cyrillic keyboard layouts. The name "Pismo" means "script" or "writing" in several Slavic languages.

**Pismo**（Писмо）は、キリル文字キーボードを使用して日本語（ひらがな・カタカナ）を入力できるキーボードアプリケーションです。「Pismo」という名前は、多くのスラブ語で「文字」や「書き物」を意味します。

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### General

<details>
<summary><strong>Q: What is Pismo?</strong></summary>

Pismo is an input method that allows you to type Japanese characters using a Cyrillic keyboard layout. It converts Cyrillic character combinations into Japanese Hiragana and Katakana.

Pismoは、キリル文字キーボードレイアウトを使用して日本語を入力できる入力メソッドです。キリル文字の組み合わせを日本語のひらがな・カタカナに変換します。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q: Who is this app for?</strong></summary>

- Japanese speakers learning Slavic languages
- Cyrillic keyboard users learning Japanese
- Researchers of Eastern Orthodox Christianity and Church Slavonic
- Linguists and language enthusiasts
- Anyone interested in the connection between Cyrillic and Japanese scripts

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q: Is this app free?</strong></summary>

Yes, Pismo is completely free and open source. There are no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

</details>

### Privacy & Security

<details>
<summary><strong>Q: Does Pismo collect my data?</strong></summary>

**No.** Pismo does not collect, store, or transmit any user data. All text processing happens locally on your device.

- No keystroke logging
- No network transmission
- No analytics or tracking
- No personal information collection

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q: Why does the keyboard need "Full Access"?</strong></summary>

Pismo works **without** Full Access. Full Access is only needed for optional features. The basic keyboard functionality works completely offline without any special permissions.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q: Is my typing data sent to any server?</strong></summary>

**Absolutely not.** All conversion from Cyrillic to Japanese happens entirely on your device. The app has no network capabilities and cannot send data anywhere.

</details>

### Technical

<details>
<summary><strong>Q: How do I install the keyboard?</strong></summary>

**iOS/iPadOS:**
1. Download Pismo from the App Store
2. Open Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards
3. Tap "Add New Keyboard..."
4. Select "Pismo" from the list
5. Choose your preferred Cyrillic layout

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q: How do I switch between keyboards?</strong></summary>

While typing, tap the globe icon (🌐) on your keyboard to switch between installed keyboards.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q: The keyboard is not appearing. What should I do?</strong></summary>

1. Ensure Pismo is installed from the App Store
2. Go to Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards
3. Remove and re-add the Pismo keyboard
4. Restart your device if the issue persists

</details>
