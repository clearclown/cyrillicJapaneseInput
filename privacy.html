🔒 Your Privacy is Protected

Pismo does NOT collect any data. Your keystrokes, text, and personal information never leave your device. We believe privacy is a fundamental right.

Data Collection

Pismo is designed with privacy as a core principle. We do NOT collect:

  • Keystroke data or typed text
  • Personal information
  • Device identifiers
  • Usage analytics
  • Location data
  • Contacts or calendar data
  • Any other user data

Data Storage

All app preferences and settings are stored locally on your device using standard iOS mechanisms. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not accessible to us
  • Is not synced to any server
  • Is deleted when you uninstall the app

Data Sharing

We do not share any data because we do not collect any data. There are:

  • No third-party analytics services
  • No advertising networks
  • No data brokers
  • No cloud synchronization

Keyboard Permissions

Pismo functions as a keyboard extension on iOS. You may be asked about "Full Access" during setup:

  • Full Access is NOT required for basic functionality
  • The keyboard works completely offline
  • No network connections are made by the keyboard
  • Your typing is processed entirely on-device

Children's Privacy

Pismo is safe for users of all ages. Since we do not collect any data from anyone, this includes children under 13 years of age. Our app:

  • Contains no age-restricted content
  • Has no social features
  • Collects no information from children or adults

Open Source

Pismo is open-source software. You can review our code on GitHub to verify our privacy practices.

Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will:

  • Update this page with the new policy
  • Update the "Last Updated" date below
  • Include the changes in app update notes

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy:
