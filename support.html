Frequently Asked Questions

How do I install the keyboard?
  1. Download Pismo from the App Store
  2. Open Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards
  3. Tap "Add New Keyboard..."
  4. Select "Pismo" from the list
  5. Choose your preferred Cyrillic layout
Does Pismo collect my data?
No. Pismo does not collect, store, or transmit any user data. All text processing happens locally on your device. See our Privacy Policy for details.
Why does the keyboard ask for "Full Access"?
Pismo works without Full Access. Full Access is only needed for optional features. The basic keyboard functionality works completely offline without any special permissions.
How do I switch between keyboards?
While typing, tap the globe icon (🌐) on your keyboard to switch between installed keyboards.
Is Pismo free?
Yes! Pismo is completely free and open source. There are no in-app purchases, subscriptions, or ads.
The keyboard isn't appearing. What should I do?
  1. Ensure Pismo is installed from the App Store
  2. Go to Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards
  3. Remove and re-add the Pismo keyboard
  4. Restart your device if the issue persists
